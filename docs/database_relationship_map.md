# Database Relationship Map

## Core POS and Restaurant Relationships

- `users 1..* roles` through `model_has_roles`
- `roles *..* permissions` through `role_has_permissions`
- `users 1..0..1 staff_profiles`
- `staff_profiles 1..* shift_schedules`
- `staff_profiles 1..* attendance`
- `staff_profiles 1..* staff_performance`
- `staff_profiles 1..* staff_commissions`
- `staff_shifts 1..* shift_schedules`
- `customers 1..* orders`
- `customers 1..* reservations`
- `tables 1..* reservations`
- `tables 1..* orders`
- `tables *..1 floor_plans`
- `tables *..0..1 tables` via `parent_table_id`
- `reservations 1..* reservation_requests`
- `reservation_requests *..1 users` through `acknowledged_by` and `completed_by`
- `orders 1..* order_items`
- `orders 1..* order_status_history`
- `orders 1..0..1 invoices`
- `orders *..0..1 discounts` through `discount_id`
- `orders *..0..1 customers`
- `orders *..0..1 tables`
- `orders *..0..1 users` through `created_by`
- `order_items *..1 menu_items`
- `order_items *..* menu_modifiers` through `order_item_modifiers`
- `invoices 1..* payments`
- `payments 1..* refunds`
- `payments *..0..1 users` through `processed_by`
- `refunds *..0..1 users` through `processed_by`

## Menu and Recipe Relationships

- `menu_categories 1..* menu_items`
- `menu_items *..* menu_modifiers` through `menu_item_modifiers`
- `menu_items 1..* recipes`
- `recipes *..* ingredients` through `recipe_ingredients`
- `menu_combos *..* menu_items` through `menu_combo_items`

## Inventory and Purchasing Relationships

- `suppliers 1..* ingredients`
- `suppliers 1..* purchase_orders`
- `purchase_orders 1..* purchase_order_items`
- `purchase_order_items *..1 ingredients`
- `ingredients 1..* stock_movements`
- `ingredients 1..* wastage`
- `ingredients 1..* replenishment_requests`
- `stock_movements *..0..1 users` through `created_by`
- `wastage *..0..1 users` through `reported_by`
- `replenishment_requests *..0..1 users` through `requested_by`

## Audit and Notification Relationships

- `users 1..* audit_logs` through `user_id`
- `audit_logs` also uses polymorphic `auditable_type` and `auditable_id`
- `users 1..* staff_notifications`
- `staff_notifications` uses polymorphic `related_type` and `related_id`

## Counts and Integrity

- Public tables: 59
- Foreign keys: 57
- Orphaned foreign-key records: 0
- Duplicate logical identifiers: 0
- Soft-delete columns are present on most operational tables through `deleted_at`.
- Archive columns are present on orders, reservations, and KOT tickets through `archived_at`.

## Notable Schema Limitations

- Reservation `end_time` is `time without time zone`, so late-night reservations wrap past midnight.
- Inventory has no opening-stock or stock-snapshot table.
- Customer classification is stored as `customer_type` with values `registered` and `walk_in`; no separate VIP/Regular/Guest lifecycle fields exist.
- Orders do not reference reservations or dining sessions.
- Kitchen tickets are not generated by the current seed data.
- Hotel entities are absent from the schema.
